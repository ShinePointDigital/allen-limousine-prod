---
name: Company profile settings
description: Where public-facing phone, email, and service area belong and how they relate to marketing copy.
---

Store business phone, contact email, and service area as structured company-profile settings, separate from editorial `SiteContent`. Public contact surfaces should read the same resolved profile so administrator updates stay consistent.

**Why:** Operational contact details change independently of homepage prose. Keeping them separate prevents a text-content edit from silently leaving public contact links stale.

**How to apply:** Add future business contact fields to the structured settings profile and public response. Keep narrative headlines and promotional copy in `SiteContent`.
